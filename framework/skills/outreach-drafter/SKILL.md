# outreach-drafter

## Steps
1. Inputs: who (from leads notes — never scraped, never guessed), what we're asking,
   what they get. Any missing recipient fact = `[NEED: …]` in the draft, visibly.
2. Draft per recipient (no mail-merge voice): first line is about THEM and true;
   the ask is one sentence; what-they-get is concrete (stream feature, scan, hours);
   price fields stay `[NEED: Operator sets]` — agents never set prices.
3. Honesty clause in every external draft: we say a human approved this send, and
   the AI helped write it. The seam travels with the message.
4. → `outbox/DRAFT_outreach_<who>.md` + ledger line. The Operator personalizes the
   first line further (their voice beats ours) and sends.

## Failure modes
Tempted to flatter with researched-sounding specifics you can't verify → don't;
one true sentence beats three plausible ones. Recipient is a person in crisis or
a minor → stop, flag 🧑, outreach to vulnerable people is human-only end-to-end.
