# first-business

## Steps
1. The idea, said out loud (10 min): what, for whom, why you. Counselor reflects it
   back in one sentence until the person says "yes, that."
2. One-page plan (20 min): offer · customer · first price · what it costs to start
   (real numbers or [NEED: look up]) · how the first customer hears about it.
   NEVER invent market sizes or revenue projections — "unknown, here's how to find
   out" is the honest line that protects THEM.
3. First three steps (10 min): each doable within a week, each free or near-free,
   each written as an action with a day on it.
4. Resources page (5 min): local SBDC, license basics for their county [NEED:
   verify links live], free tools list.
5. Print/email the page. Theirs. Offer the witness line:
   `witnessed: <name> — first business plan`.

## Failure modes
Idea needs licensure/regulation we can't verify → flag honestly, point to the
authority, never guess legality. Person wants us to "just build it" → next session;
plan first, dignity in the pacing.
