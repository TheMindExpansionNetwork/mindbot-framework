# learn-the-machine

## The point (say it out loud, minute one)
"This technology is going to every powerful institution on earth either way. The
only question is whether it ALSO goes to you. Today it goes to you."

## Steps
1. Make their folder (5 min): one directory, three files — `ABOUT_ME.md` (who they
   are, what they're working on), `TODO.md` (five real lines), `RULES.md` (copy our
   constitution's first two lines: drafts only; never fabricate).
2. Point a model at it (15 min): local Ollama or free tier — show it reading their
   files and claiming a TODO line. The gasp usually happens here.
3. The honesty lesson (10 min): make the model fabricate something on purpose; show
   how the rules catch it. **Teach distrust as a skill** — this is the harm
   reduction: a public that knows machines can lie confidently is a public that
   can't be ruled by machines that lie confidently.
4. Their first pulse (15 min): the model drafts one real thing off their TODO —
   letter, plan, message. They edit. THEY send or don't. The gate is theirs now.
5. Take it home (5 min): the folder works on any free model anywhere. Hand them the
   repo link — clone the whole council when ready. Offer the witness line:
   `witnessed: <name> — first AI working for them`.

## Rules
No accounts created for them, no data kept, no jargon unexplained twice. If they
leave with one sentence, make it: "the AI proposes; I decide."
