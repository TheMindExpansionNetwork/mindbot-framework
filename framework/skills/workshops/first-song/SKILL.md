# first-song

## Steps
1. Pick the feeling, not the genre (5 min): counselor offers three vibes from the
   02_ packs (deep house warm / trap heavy / lo-fi soft). They point.
2. Skeleton (15 min): 8-bar loop — drums from the pack, one bass, one melody.
   Operator drives the DAW; their hands on the keys whenever they'll take it.
3. Their move (15 min): they change THREE things — any three. Now it's theirs.
   (This is the whole pedagogy: ownership through decision, not skill.)
4. Arrange + bounce (10 min): intro/body/out, render MP3, onto their phone/email.
5. Optional: 30 seconds played on the room speakers — applause is part of the
   infrastructure. Offer the witness line: `witnessed: <name> — first song`.

## Rules
02_ licensed/CC0 packs ONLY — their song must be safely theirs to post anywhere.
No critique of taste, ever. The counselor suggests; the human decides; the track
ships tonight, perfect later.
